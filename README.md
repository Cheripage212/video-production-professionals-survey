#  Video Production Professionals Survey

Welcome to the **Video Production Professionals Survey** repository.

This project is part of an initiative to identify skilled video editors and content creators who can contribute to an advanced AI research project focused on digital media.

---

##  About the Project

We are looking for professionals experienced in video editing tools such as:

-  Shotcut  
-  OpenShot  
-  Lightworks  

Selected contributors will assist in building and refining high-quality video datasets used for AI training and evaluation.

---

##  What We Are Evaluating

This survey helps assess:

- Video editing and production skills  
- Familiarity with editing software  
- Understanding of storytelling and visual composition  
- Ability to work on AI-related creative tasks  

---

##  Required Skills

- Video cutting and trimming  
- Transitions and visual effects  
- Audio syncing and enhancement  
- Color correction and grading  
- Exporting videos in multiple formats
